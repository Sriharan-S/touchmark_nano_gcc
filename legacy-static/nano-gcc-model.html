The Nano GCC Model

Big GCC Thinking. Nano-Sized Starting Point.

You don't need hundreds of employees and a major infrastructure investment to begin building capability in India. Start with a focused team, prove the business case, and scale progressively.

What You Can Build

Capability shaped around your goal — not a template.

A Nano GCC is defined by what you are trying to prove, and sized accordingly.

  • AI deployment and engineering teams
  • Product engineering and R&D pods
  • Deep-tech development capability
  • Rapid prototyping and market validation squads
  • Technology support functions
  • Selected back-office and operational capability

The Journey

Prove it before you scale it.

Step 01Rapid Innovation
Step 02Quick Prototyping
Step 03Validation

If an experiment succeeds, you scale it. If it doesn't, you exit or change direction — without carrying the cost structure of a conventional large-scale setup.

Over time, a single successful Nano GCC can grow into a larger capability center, or you can run multiple Nano GCC units across different goals.
