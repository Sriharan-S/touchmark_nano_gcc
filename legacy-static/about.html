About & Vision

Built From Tamil Nadu. Designed For The World.

Touchmark Nano GCC Hub exists to close a gap: global technology companies want agile access to talent and innovation capacity in India, but the traditional route — a full-scale Global Capability Center — asks for too much commitment too early.

Our Philosophy

Agility over scale.

A company may have a promising idea but not want to spend significant time and capital building a large team before knowing whether the idea will work.

Through the Nano GCC ecosystem, companies prototype faster, validate earlier, experiment with lower investment, and accelerate their go-to-market journey.

Our Vision

Start small, innovate fast, and build scalable capabilities from Tamil Nadu.

Touchmark Nano GCC Hub enables global companies to do exactly that — while creating meaningful global industry opportunities for the state's talent and institutions.

This isn't recruitment, outsourcing, or conventional GCC consulting. It's an agile global innovation and capability-building ecosystem, built from Tamil Nadu, for the world.

Why Tamil Nadu

Talent, institutions and innovation — in one ecosystem.

Tamil Nadu combines a large, diverse technical talent pool with a dense network of engineering and research institutions, and a fast-maturing innovation ecosystem.

Touchmark's role is to make that ecosystem legible and accessible to global companies — connecting the right capability to the right requirement, without either side having to navigate it alone.
