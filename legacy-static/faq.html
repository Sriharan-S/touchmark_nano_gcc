FAQ

Questions, Answered.

The things companies and institutions ask most often before starting a conversation.

How is this different from outsourcing or staffing?

A Nano GCC is your own dedicated capability unit, built around your goals — not a shared staffing pool. It's designed to grow into your own team's extension, not a vendor relationship.

What does institution membership actually include?

Access to relevant opportunities as they arise — internships, MoUs, Centres of Excellence, R&D collaborations — matched to your institution's strengths. It is not direct access to a contact database.

How quickly can a company get started?

Timelines depend on the scope of the requirement. Start a conversation and we'll help you understand what's realistic for your specific goal.

Do we have to commit to a fixed team size upfront?

No. Nano GCCs are designed to start small — typically 5 to 100 professionals — and scale based on validated outcomes, not a fixed commitment.

Can a Nano GCC grow into a full-scale GCC?

Yes. Over time, a successful Nano GCC can scale into a larger capability center, or a company can run multiple Nano GCC units across different goals.

What does it cost?

Costs depend on team size, focus area and scope. Start a conversation with us for a proposal tailored to your requirement.

Which institutions and regions are involved?

The initial focus is Tamil Nadu, working with a curated set of partner institutions. See the Ecosystem page for partner categories.
