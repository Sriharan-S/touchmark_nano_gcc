Bring Global Industry Opportunity To Your Campus.

The Touchmark Nano GCC ecosystem connects institutions to real international industry engagement — internships, collaborations, and Global Capability Center exposure for students and faculty.

What Membership Can Open Up

Real engagement, matched to your strengths.

  • International and international-remote internships
  • Overseas placement opportunities
  • Industry–academia MoUs
  • Centres of Excellence and corporate-supported laboratories
  • R&D and product-development collaborations
  • Access to real industry problem statements

What Membership Is Not

A facilitated relationship, not a contact list.

Membership connects your institution to opportunities and collaborations — it is not direct access to a database of overseas company contacts. Touchmark remains the facilitator and mapping partner throughout.
